What it catches

The clauses companies
hope you skip

Most people scroll past thousands of words of legal text. TOS Guardian uses AI to surface what actually matters, ranked by how much it should concern you.

High Concern
Forced Arbitration Clause
You waive your right to sue in court. Disputes go to a private arbitrator — usually paid by the company.
High Concern
Data Sold to Third Parties
The service can sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
High Concern
Auto-Renewal Trap
Free trials silently convert to paid subscriptions without clear notice to the user.
Worth Noting
Content Ownership Claim
The platform claims a license to use, modify, or distribute anything you upload.
Worth Noting
Unilateral Terms Changes
The company can change these terms at any time, with or without notifying you first.
Minor Point
Broad Liability Waiver
The service disclaims responsibility for virtually any harm caused by their platform.
